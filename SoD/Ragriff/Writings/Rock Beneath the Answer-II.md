:::lang en

For the governments of Silvattia and Lucatreslica, SMA was not their only option. In fact, around 1360, [[M.A.R]] had also actively reached out to both of them (this was in fact one of the reasons why Serrano hoped to formalize CHANGE), but due to concerns over maintaining control of their own territorial integrity, they gave no definitive reply until SMA submitted their application. For the subjects of the southern nations, having a magic association based in their homeland was当然值得高兴的好事，但显然希望向外继续扩张的M.A.R.的管理者们以及一些颇有野心的，[[Ragriff]] 的魔法师们并不这么认为。 naturally cause for celebration, but the managers of M.A.R., who hoped to continue expanding outward, and certain ambitious mages of [[Ragriff]] clearly did not share this view.

To their delight, the young SMA quickly fell into conflict. This conflict involved a wide range of issues with complex causes and consequences, and its course has largely become difficult to trace today. One prevalent account holds that 風語, who was disinclined to pursue fame and fortune, got into a dispute with the other three. Even though he had already lost his voice at the time, out of respect for him, the SMA still hesitated in its direction of advance. What can be confirmed is that, following this incident, the SMA relocated its headquarters to [[Puteoli]] in the north of 希爾瓦提亞; the other three mostly stayed in 澤斯卡, no longer interfering in the SMA's internal affairs. 維薩第拉斯 gradually took over management authority, and thereafter, alone, continuously led the SMA toward glory.

The events involving SMA date back far and are fraught with uncertainties. The account above is the commonly accepted version. As for whatever errors it may contain, that is for "you" to uncover...

对于希尔瓦提亚与卢卡特斯里卡政府而言，SMA并不是唯一的选择。事实上，在1360年前后，[[M.A.R|拉格里弗魔法协会]] 也曾积极联络过二者（实际上这也是促使塞拉诺希望将CHANGE正规化的原因之一），出于对自身国土完整的控制性考虑，他们并未给出明确的回复，直至SMA投递了他们的申请。对于南方诸国的臣民来说，拥有了居于本土的魔法协会当然是值得高兴的好事，但显然希望向外继续扩张的M.A.R.的管理者们以及一些颇有野心的，[[Ragriff|拉格里弗]] 的魔法师们并不这么认为。

让他们感到高兴的是，年轻的SMA很快陷入了争斗中。这场争斗涉及甚广，因果复杂，其经过如今已大多难辨，一种主流的说法是：不愿追名逐利的风语与其他三人发生了争执，即使当时的他已经失去了话语权，出于对他的尊重，SMA仍在前进方向上发生了迟疑。可以确定的是，在此事件之后，SMA将总部迁移到了位于希尔瓦提亚北部的[[Puteoli|普特奥利]] ，其余三人大多留在泽斯卡，不再干涉SMA内部事务，维萨第拉斯逐渐掌握了管理权，此后独自一人不断带领SMA走向辉煌。
