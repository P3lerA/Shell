:::lang en

This epic of [[Hillkind]] [[Origin]] has been passed down orally from generation to generation by the mountain ministers. This book was compiled and edited by an unknown author, written in [[Sindamm]].

---

I sing the praises of that hero and his descendants, the family that suffered unjust treatment.

He was the first 山臣 to leave the plain and journey upstream, the 山臣 cursed for slaying a god.

Tormented between the plains and peaks, they would forever remember that night.

All things bear tears, the things of this mortal world touch the heart…

---

The poem records in detail how Gelolade, a warrior among the hill subjects, challenged Gedo, the god of fate at the time, and succeeded in slaying the god. However, the reward for the hill subjects' courage was an endless curse—the Gelolade clan suffered even harsher punishment for their transgression, and this is the origin of [[Hillax]].

:::lang zh

有关[[Hillkind|山臣]] 一族[[Origin|起源]] 的史诗，代代相传于山臣的口中。本书由某位不知名的作家整理编辑而成，以[[Sindamm|辛丹姆语]] 写就。

---

**隹咏厥雄，及厥孙子；**

**哀厥宗族，罹此非辜。**

**厥始违原，泝水徂山；**

**方于臣山，畔上用诅。**

**在原在岳，荐罹忧瘁；**

**永怀厥夕，世不敢忘。**

**百物有泪，民命靡常；**

**凡属有死，爰感人心。**


---

诗中详尽记载了山臣中的勇士格洛拉特向彼时的命运之神戈多发出决斗，并成功弑神的故事。然而，山臣勇气的回报却是无尽的诅咒，格洛拉特一族因僭越受到了更严重的惩罚，此即[[Hillax|山灵]] 的由来。

:::
