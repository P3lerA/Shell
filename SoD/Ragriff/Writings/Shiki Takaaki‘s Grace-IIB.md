:::lang en

Ferdinand Cysindera (the original name of the ArchMage [[Ferdinand C. Argos]]) was an ordinary fellow — that's how most people in the little town where he lived would describe him. He would slip out of town alone on a leisurely afternoon and return by evening; no one knew, and no one cared where he'd gone. Had it not been for the sudden appearance of an evil mage who destroyed everything, perhaps he would have lived out his days peacefully in that town.

Many critics have observed that Ferdinand "lacks emotion and has no ambition befitting a [[Fandrare]]" — and this assessment is well-founded. The attack took from Ferdinand both parents who hadn't loved him particularly deeply and the small town where he had lived for over a decade; during his journey north to pursue studies, he endured every hardship one might imagine. For a boy who had lived an overly peaceful life for fifteen years, that was enough to erase most of his desires.

Under any circumstances, the causal chain had already formed. Because within the boy's blood, the Hillkind smelled the scent of their own kin, Ferdinand was able to ascend the Hillkind Mountains. Because within him he saw a genius capable of turning the rotten into the miraculous, the lord of the mountains [[Ourea]] decided to take in this outsider as a disciple. Synomemn tried to save himself by attacking the town, but it was this very act that granted Ferdinand the power to kill him.

From this perspective, while Shiki Takaaki is indispensable, it seems [[The Nameless Prophet]] is what truly marks the beginning of this whole farce. However, had it not been for Ferdinand's guidance, Epypsyny would never have gained the power to rival the Sunglow Tower (i.e., the Reboot), and everything would have fallen into void. And if not for Synomemn's death, who else could have severed this cycle of suffering…

:::lang zh

斐迪南•凯辛德拉（大魔法师[[Ferdinand C. Argos|斐迪南•C•欧戈斯]] 的原名）是个平凡的家伙，在他生活的小镇，大部分人都会这么形容他。他会在悠闲的午后独自一人离开小镇，在傍晚时归来，没有人知道，也没有人在意他去了哪里。如不是突如其来的邪恶魔法师摧毁了一切，也许他会在小镇中平静终老。

许多评论家认为斐迪南“缺乏情感，没有身为[[Fandrare|范德拉]] 的野心”，这是有迹可循的。那场袭击让斐迪南失去了并不算多爱他的双亲与生活了十多年的小镇，在北上求学的过程中，他承受了可以想象的苦难，对于一个度过了过于平静的十五年的生活的少年而言，那足以抹去他的大部分渴望。

无论如何，因果已经构成。因为在少年的血液中，山臣嗅到了同族人的气息，斐迪南得以登上山臣群山。因为在他的眼中看到了可以化腐朽为神奇的天才，群山之主[[Ourea|俄瑞亚]] 决定收容这个异族人为徒。希诺蒙试图拯救自己而袭击了小镇，但正是这个行为赋予了斐迪南杀死他的力量。

从这个角度出发，四季高岭虽不可或缺，但[[The Nameless Prophet|无名的预言家]] 似乎才是这场闹剧的起点。然而，倘若没有费迪南的引导，玊初无法拥有匹敌晨曦高塔（即重启）的力量，一切都将陷入虚无。如不是希诺蒙之死，又有谁来将这苦难的循环斩断呢…
