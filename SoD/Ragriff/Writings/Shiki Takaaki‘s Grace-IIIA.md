:::lang en

Decades after the attack, out of curiosity, 四季高岭 took control of 希诺蒙, and this control lasted longer than the combined total of all her previous exertions over others.

Professor Shinomon would often realize that he was being controlled by something beyond his own will. Initially, he thought it was some kind of [[Puppetry]]. When this control became more and more frequent and completely impossible to resist, this seasoned magician realized it was a force beyond his own comprehension.

He told almost no one the reason why, but Professor Shinom resigned from his job and began traveling north. He wanted to satisfy his wish before losing control of this body: to move to [[SingLand]], the place he had longed for as a boy. Unfortunately, even when the soul known as Shinom Laplace dissipated, his body had never once set foot in SingLand. — Shijigaoling went there once, and she didn't much like it.

He lingered on like this, enduring over a hundred years of suffering. Everything happened. The rise of [[Argos]], [[ALLGOES]]'s ascension to godhood, war, creation, destruction. Then in 3140, Shiki Kōryū dragged that broken body of his and knocked on the door of the wooden cabin.

:::lang zh

袭击一事的数十年后，出于好奇，四季高岭控制了希诺蒙，而这次控制的时长超过了她此前在别人身上的总和。

希诺蒙教授时常能意识到自己被自身意志以外的东西控制着，最初，他以为那是某种[[Puppetry|傀儡术]] 。 在这种控制愈来愈频繁，并且完全无法抵抗后，这位老练的魔法师认识到那是一种自己认知外的力量。

几乎没有告诉任何人缘由，希诺蒙教授辞去工作，开始向北旅行。他希望在失去对这具躯体的控制之前满足自己的愿望：迁居[[SingLand|新洲]] ，那是少年的他向往的地方。可惜，直到名为希诺蒙•拉普拉斯的灵魂消散时，其躯体也未曾踏足新洲。——四季高岭去过那里，她不太喜欢。

他就如此苟延残喘着，度过了百余年的痛苦时光。一切都发生了。[[Argos|欧戈斯]] 的崛起，[[ALLGOES|阿尔戈斯]] 登神，战争，创造，毁灭。直到3140年，四季高岭拖着那副残破不堪的身体敲响了木屋的门。
