If the story were to end here, *Rock Beneath the Answer* would merely be a continuation of the previous tale. In truth, what Morgras witnessed was not the complete truth. The SMA quartet has their own complete story, and should possess a complete will.

[[Versatilys Sellano]] is a genius politician who entered service midway through, a mage capable of transforming the rotten into the magical — and yet Morgras deluded himself into believing he was completely unaware of it all. The truth is, he had full knowledge of everything the three had done, and maintained absolute silence, knowing that SMA at that time could not withstand any turmoil. For a nobler cause, Sellano chose this painful silence, watching the old friend who misconstrued him drift farther and farther away.

In the game, obtaining this information should be extraordinarily difficult. That is how the story was meant to be. Truth, pain, the past — all buried deep beneath the stone, nowhere to be found.
