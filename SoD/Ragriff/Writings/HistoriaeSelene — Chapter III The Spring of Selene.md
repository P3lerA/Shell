A historical book jointly compiled by historical societies of Althea and SingLand, comprehensive in content and meticulous in analysis. The standard edition is in Lingua-Franca, though those who wish to read it in Elvish can also do so.

This is the "The Spring of Selene" chapter of the Selene volume, a subject on which historians from both countries have expended considerable effort.

Chapter 1 Prelude

If we speak fairly, the House of Meruwa has produced countless geniuses. We need not specifically name any of them — anyone with even a passing interest in art or history would know their names by heart.

Perhaps due to the inherent flaws of all hereditary systems, the descendants of those once talented ancestors degenerated into pleasure-seeking drunkards and lechers.

In 3124, after the ascension of Ashidaka de Meruwa, all of this was tragically pushed to its extreme. Ashidaka I seemed to have no interest whatsoever in magical research or the management of state affairs; if he had leisure time, he preferred to go hunting, or to savor delicacies from various nations, and his extravagant lifestyle caused him to become extremely obese.

The ugly are always more sensitive about their image, and the old ministers of the former dynasty are always subjecting him to annoying interference. Ashitaka is not stupid—on the contrary, he possesses the uniquely cruel cunning of a fat man, and he is secretly scheming a coup.

After an ordinary session of state affairs ended, a madman suddenly burst into the parliament hall, his face shrouded in cloth, screaming as he unleashed Cinders of Tarragona—a devastating fire spell that incinerated numerous legislators. In his frenzy, he turned the flames upon himself and burned to ash. This catastrophic disaster would later come to be known as the "Flower Moon Coup".

Ashidaka I happened to not be present that day.

How could a madman slip through such tight security? How could he walk right into the parliament in broad daylight without a care? And why did he burn himself alive?

The answer is obvious—it was nothing more than a carefully orchestrated conspiracy by Ashidaka I. He used it to eliminate his numerous opponents, and on this pretext executed the former High Sheriff, replacing him with his own confidant—Piron Yugen.

We need not dwell on Ashidaka's conduct after seizing power. Ashidaka has an odd habit—he is always eager to give nicknames to his subordinates, such as "Living Trash" Piron Yugen and "the Brain" Meigaomei Serafet. Regardless, he relies on these cronies to keep Selene running—a strained operation at best.

When force is applied to an object, the greater the force exerted, the greater the reactive force—this is a natural law. It has been proven that this law applies equally to the societies of intelligent beings. After the Flower Moon Coup, Selene was seething with undercurrents. Those who had studied the politics of SingLand or Althea and believed in republicanism spontaneously formed their own organization—the House of Republicans—whose leader was Professor Drefort Erdo of the University of Anvergne.

Chp1 前奏

如果公正的说，墨卢瓦家族曾经出现过无数的天才，我们不必特意说出某些人的名字，任何一个或是对艺术，或是对历史有所兴趣的人都会对他们的名字如数家珍。

也许是因为一切世袭制度的弊端，曾经天才傍身的先祖的后代们却退化成了享乐的酒徒和色鬼。

3124年，阿席达卡·德·墨卢瓦即位后，这一切被可悲的推向了顶点。阿席达卡一世似乎无论是对魔法研究还是国是制定都毫无兴趣，如果有闲，他更爱去打猎，或者品尝各国的美食，奢靡的生活让他变得极为肥胖。

丑人总是对自己的形象更加敏感，前朝的旧臣又总是对他施以令人厌烦的掣肘，阿席达卡并不蠢，相反他有着胖子独有的，残忍的精明，他暗中策划着一场政变。

在某次普通的国务会议结束后，一个疯子突然闯进了国会大厅内之内，他蒙着头巾，尖叫着施展着塔拉戈纳之烬，一种威力极大的火魔法，将大量议员活活烧死后，他尖叫着将自己也烧成了灰烬。这场悲剧性的灾祸后来被称为“花月政变”。

阿席达卡一世当日恰好不在场。

一个疯子如何能穿越重重安保呢？如何能在白日大摇大摆的走入国会之中呢？他又为什么将自己活活烧死？

答案是显而易见的，这不过是阿席达卡一世一场精心策划的阴谋罢了。他借此除掉了自己的诸多对手，并以此为理由处死了原最高警司，并代之以自己的亲信——皮隆·尤根。

我们无需赘述阿席达卡掌控权力后的种种行径。阿席达卡有个奇怪的癖好，他总是很乐意给自己的下属取绰号，像是“活垃圾”皮隆·尤根，“智囊”美高梅·塞拉菲特，无论如何，他依靠这些亲信维持着塞勒涅的运转——很勉强的。

当某种力量加诸于某一物体时，所施加的力越大，反弹的力量也就越大——这是一条自然法则。事实已经证明，这一法则对智慧体之社会亦然适用。花月政变发生后，塞勒涅已暗潮涌动，曾了解过新洲或是阿莱西亚政治，笃信着共和制的人们自发性的组建了自己的组织——共和者之家，他们的领导人即时任奥弗娜大学的教授，德雷福·埃尔多。
