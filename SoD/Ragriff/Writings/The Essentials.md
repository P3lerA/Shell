:::lang en

A book written by [[Ryne Lynne]] covering basic magical knowledge, completed around 1200. It has now become the standard introductory text for the Continental Magic Association, with the original manuscript lost.

---

---

The book documents the costs and regrets of studying magic, as well as the fundamental principles of wielding it — the magnum opus of ArchMage Ryne Lynne in their later years, during the budding age.
