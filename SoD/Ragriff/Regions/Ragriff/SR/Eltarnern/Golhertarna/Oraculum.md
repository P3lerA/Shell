:::lang en

*The Tree* of the elves, located within the city of Golhertarna. In the elvish tongue it means *Tree of Prophecy*, and is a blessing from the Textor Fati [[Mnemosyne]] to the elves in the distant age of antiquity.

Guiding the fate of the elven race until it faded to pallor, now existing only in the elves' currency and insignias.

Commonly noted as *the Tree.*


:::
