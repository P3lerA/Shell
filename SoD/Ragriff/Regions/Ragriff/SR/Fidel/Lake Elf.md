:::lang en

Located in the northern part of South Ragriff, within the territory of Fidel. In the elven language, **ELf** means *Lake of the Elves*.

The lake water is clear, teeming with aquatic life.

The displaced [[High Elf]] who had migrated north finally stopped their wandering, swearing to restore the glory of the elven race, and thus the lake came to be called Lake Elf.

:::
