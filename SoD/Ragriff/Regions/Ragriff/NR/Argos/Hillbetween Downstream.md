:::lang en

Originating from [[Hillkind Mountains]] and flowing into [[Green Lake]], this river traverses the entire territory of Argos.

When the river flows to the foot of the mountain, the flow rate gradually slows, allowing the Argosians to obtain abundant irrigation water sources.



*The Argosians may be fierce fighters, but they're certainly no good at naming.*
