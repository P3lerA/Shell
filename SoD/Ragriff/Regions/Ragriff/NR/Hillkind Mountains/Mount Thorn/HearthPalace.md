:::lang en

A solemn palace perched atop the towering Vine Mountain, with most of its walls displaying dramatic, near-vertical constructions, a masterpiece of [[Hillkind]]'s architectural craftsmanship.

HearthPalace is also the palace recognized by [[Ourea]].

:::
