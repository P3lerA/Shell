:::lang en

A leaf that drifted down from the withered [[Oraculum]], its pallid part shaped like a crescent. An elf on the northward migration picked it up and kept it as some kind of keepsake. Bound by [[Preservation Magic]], it still remains half green and half pale to this day, like the gentle moon of Eltarnern in days long past.

:::
