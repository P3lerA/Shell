:::lang en

Magic is an innate talent of Sorcerers, and they can cast magics without mediums such as wand, because it flows within their blood.

Such talents often originate from a certain bloodline, for example, the magical users in [[Clan Elizabeth]] often possess [[Mnemonia]] as an [[Innate Magic]].

*How dull the world of magic would be without us.*
