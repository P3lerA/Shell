:::lang en

Fandrare, the "ArchMage", is an honorific title given to great mages, first originating from within the Elizabethan court for [[Phatherence]]. This title was actually proposed by [[Alice]], drawn from her father [[Fandrare Kyren]]'s name, because "he was a great mage”.

The former had proposed that the latter teach their skills to future generations and seek out the next Fandrare, but this suggestion did not receive Phatherence's approval. Despite this, the title ArchMages has been passed down to this day, and the term Fandrare has become a symbol of the highest point of magic.

Generally speaking, one cannot become a ArchMages without the certification of [[M.A.R]], even if he is not a mage of [[Ragriff]].

Note: This term does not refer to Sorcerers. For the distinction between Sorcerers and Mages, see [[Mage & Sorcerers]].

:::lang zh

范德拉[Fandrare]，即“大魔法师[Archmage]”，是对于强大魔法师[Mage]的尊称，最早源于伊丽莎白宫廷内对[[Phatherence|帕苏恩斯]] 的称呼。这一称呼实际上来自[[Alice|Alice]] 的提议，源于其父[[Fandrare Kyren|范德拉•修]] 的名字，因为“他是个了不起的魔法师”。

前者曾提议后者授技后人，寻找下一代范德拉，但该建议并没有得到帕苏恩斯的认同。尽管如此，大魔法师这一称呼还是流传至今，范德拉一词也成为了魔法至高点的象徽。

一般而言，大魔法师的诞生需要[[M.A.R|拉格里弗魔法协会]]的认证，即使他不是[[Ragriff|拉格里弗]] 的魔法师。

注：该词不指代魔法使[Sorcerer]。关于魔法师[Mage]与魔法使[Sorcerer]的区别，见[[Mage & Sorcerers|魔法师与魔法使]] 。
