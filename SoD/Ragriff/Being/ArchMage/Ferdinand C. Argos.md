:::lang en

Ferdinand C. Argos (2884-), the Final[[Fandrare]]. [[Hillkind]]’s magic greatly extended his lifespan, but he now shows his age.

Ferdinand's original name wasn't Argos. At sixteen, [[Synomemn Laplace]] attacked his hometown [[Angro]], and as a survivor he wandered north until reaching [[Argos]].

At that time, Argos was in the midst of a frenzy of assimilation and expansion. To gain the recognition of the Argos people, Ferdinand took to calling himself Argos as well. Most legends and anecdotes originate from this period. The name Ferdinand Argos gained widespread fame as Fandrare, while his true name has since been lost to all memory.

:::

:::lang zh

斐迪南•欧戈斯 [Ferdinand•Argos] (2884-) , 最后的[[Fandrare|范德拉]]。 [[Hillkind|山臣]]的魔法大大延长了他的寿命，但如今也已尽显老态。

斐迪南的原名并不是欧戈斯。在他十六岁时，[[Synomemn Laplace|希诺蒙•拉普拉斯]] 袭击了他的家乡 [[Angro|安格洛]]，作为幸存者一路向北流浪来到了[[Argos|欧戈斯]] 。

彼时欧戈斯正处于同化与扩张的狂潮之中，为博得欧戈斯人的认可，斐迪南便亦以欧戈斯自称。大多传说轶事发源于此，斐迪南·欧戈斯之名作为范德拉声名远在，至于其本名已无人知晓。
