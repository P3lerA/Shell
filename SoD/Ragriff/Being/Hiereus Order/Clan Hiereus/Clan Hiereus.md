:::lang en

The primordial priestly clan that witnessed the radiance of [[the Tree]] that has been led by [[Clan Elizabeth]]* since ancient times.* It means *priest* for Hiereus in the Elven tongue.

The [[Astreia]] was created by the ancestors of the clan, and many ancient mysterious rituals are still preserved today.
