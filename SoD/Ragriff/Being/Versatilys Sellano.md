:::lang en

Versatilys Sellano (1310-1387), human mage, politician, [[Sylvatia]] native, and founding initiator of [[SMA]].

Born into a noble family, Versatilys Sellano received a fine education from childhood, displaying a strong interest in magic and astonishing talent.

In his youth, Versatilys Sellano resolved to make the study of magic his life's work. However, his father's sudden death forced him to temporarily abandon his ideals and turn to politics instead. Naïve and inexperienced, he grew through countless setbacks, and by the age of thirty-two, Versatilys Sellano had become an accomplished yet corrupt politician.

Even so, he still couldn't give up his love for magic. After decades in the political world, Versatilys Sellano resolutely decided to abandon everything he had achieved in the first half of his life, gathered friends to establish a magic research association, which was the predecessor of [[SMA]].

Even after Versatilys's death, SMA continues to grow, and has become an important magical organization on par with [[M.A.R]]. That old man who loved magic would surely be overjoyed if he could see this.


*All I can offer is nothing but blood, toil, sweat, and tears.*

:::lang zh

维萨第拉斯•塞拉诺[Versatilys•Sellano] (1310-1387), 人类魔法师，政治家，[[Sylvatia|希尔瓦提亚]] 人，[[SMA|南方魔法联盟]] 最初的发起者。

出生于权贵之家，维萨第拉斯自幼便接受了良好的教育，表现出对魔法强烈的兴趣与惊人的天赋。

少年时，维萨第拉斯就立志以研究魔法作为此生的事业。然而，父亲的突然离世不得不迫使他暂时放弃理想，转而投身政治。不谙世事的他在无数挫折中成长，32岁时，维萨第拉斯就已经成为了一名杰出而肮脏的政治家。

即使如此，他依旧无法放弃对魔法的喜爱。混迹政坛数十年后，维萨第拉斯毅然决定抛弃他在前半生中取得的一切，召集好友成立了一个魔法研究同好会，即[[SMA|南方魔法联盟]] 的前身。

即使在维萨第拉斯过世后，SMA仍在不断壮大，如今已是与[[M.A.R|拉格里弗魔法协会]] 齐名的重要魔法组织。那个热爱魔法的老人如果能看到的话，一定会欣慰无比吧。


*我所能奉献的，只有热血辛劳汗水和眼泪。*
