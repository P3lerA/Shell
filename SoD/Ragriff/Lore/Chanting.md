:::lang en

Chanting is a method used to help gather and control magic power when releasing very complex or difficult spells. If the magic is relatively simple or the magician is powerful enough, all magic can be released without chanting. Even under this premise, magicians can still increase a spell's effectiveness through chanting.

:::
