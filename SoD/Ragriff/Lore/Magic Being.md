:::lang en

The so-called "Four Magic Beings". Generally refers to [[Elf]], [[Hillkind]], [[Dragon]], and [[Fallax]], races born with magic in their blood. The vast majority of the world's [[Sorcerer]]s have emerged from these four races and their descendants.

:::
