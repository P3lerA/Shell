:::lang en

Innate Magic, formally *Innatus*, is a form of magic unique to [[Sorcerer]]  — a manifestation of their magical specificity.

Most Innate Magic is bound to bloodline. The classic example is [[Clan Elizabeth]]’s [[Mnemonia]]  — which is easy enough to understand, since "magic flows in the blood of Sorcerers."

:::

:::lang zh

天赋魔法，学名 Innatus，是 [[Sorcerer|魔法使们]] 特有的魔法，是其魔力特异性的体现。

大部分天赋魔法都与血脉绑定。最经典的例子是 [[Clan Elizabeth|伊丽莎白家族]] 的 [[Mnemonia|记忆魔法]] ——这很好理解，因为“魔力就流淌在魔法使的血中”。

:::
