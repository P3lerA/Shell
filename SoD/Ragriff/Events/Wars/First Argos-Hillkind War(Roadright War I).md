:::lang en

[[Argos]] has been in long-term conflict with [[Hillkind]] since its birth.

When Argos, as a newly emerging nation that had not yet stabilized, sought to expand outward, it cast its covetous eyes upon the ambiguously defined borderlands between [[Hillkind Mountains]] and Argos.

Being long plagued by the aggressive actions of Argos, the Hillkind initiated this war — the first conflict between the two. At that time, [[ALLGOES]] was still under the tutelage of [[Ourea]].

:::

:::lang zh

[[Argos|欧戈斯]] 自诞生以来，就处于与[[Hillkind|山臣]] 的长期矛盾中。

在欧戈斯作为新兴之国尚未站稳脚跟，寻求向外扩张之际，就窥求于[[Hillkind Mountains|山臣群山]] 与欧戈斯之间暧昧不清的交界地。

长期受欧戈斯[Argos]的侵略性行为烦扰不堪的山臣籍此发起战争，即两者之间的第一次战争。彼时，[[ALLGOES|阿尔戈斯]] 尚在[[Ourea|俄瑞亚]] 门下。

:::
