:::lang en

A four-legged, two-winged flying species—the romantic essence of Western fantasy.

In Ragriff, Dragons are not such a common species, but people won't be surprised to see a Dragon in a tavern either.

Dragons have relatively long lifespans. Without specific notation (such as [[Fire Dragon]], [[Blue Dragon]], or other common subspecies), lifespans generally reach 200-500 years, while individual long-lived species (such as [[Seawyrm]]) can even reach several thousand years.

Unless otherwise noted, Dragons cannot communicate using human language. A "Dragon Speaker" who can communicate with Dragons is a rare talent.

Dragons often perceive prolonged eye contact as an unfriendly act.

:::lang zh

四足两翼的飞行种，西式幻想的浪漫所在。

在拉格里弗，龙并不是那么常见的物种，不过大家也不会因为在酒馆里见到龙而感到惊讶。

龙的寿命较长。如无注明（如[[Fire Dragon|火龙]] ，[[Blue Dragon|蓝龙]] 等常见亚种）寿命一般可达200-500年，个别长生种（如[[Seawyrm|海龙]] ）甚至可达数千年。

如无注明，龙无法使用人的语言交流。能与龙交流的“龙语者”是一种罕见的天赋。

与龙长时间注视往往会被龙认为是不友好的行为。
