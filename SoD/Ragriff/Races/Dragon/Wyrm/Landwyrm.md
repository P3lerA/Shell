:::lang en

A gentle giant herbivore species, with adults measuring 30–45 meters in body length and standing 20–35 meters in height.

Occasionally found in [[Coimbra Archipelago]] and the southeast of [[Pandemoniac]].

:::

:::lang zh

温和的巨型素食物种，成年体身长在30-45米之间，身高可达20-35米。

偶见于[[Coimbra Archipelago|科英布拉群岛]] 与[[Pandemoniac|潘德莫尼亚]] 东南部。

:::
