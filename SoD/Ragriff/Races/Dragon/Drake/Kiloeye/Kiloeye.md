:::lang en

True to its name, the indescribable drake with a thousand eyes and a thousand legs is mostly found in [[Pandemoniac]].

Body length 3–4 m, flat body, poor fliers, generally float at low altitudes.

Kiloeye possesses [[Dragon Sight]] capable of interfering with the mind: disturbing the sequence of memories in living beings upon which it gazes, causing them to fall into chaos. Since Kiloeye's eyes are distributed across its entire body, it is an extremely dangerous creature for adventurers whose mental barriers are not strong enough.

Compared to its magical abilities, Kiloeye's physical hunting capability is relatively weak. However, considering that the disturbed prey virtually lacks any ability to resist, this may be a reasonable evolutionary direction.

*Take it slow, this won't hurt…*

正如其名，拥有千目与千足的不可名状的亚龙，大多分布于[[Pandemoniac|潘德莫尼亚]] 。

身长3-4m, 身形扁平，飞行能力较弱，一般在低空漂浮。

千目拥有可以干扰心智的[[Dragon Sight|龙视魔法]] ：扰动注视到活物的记忆顺序，使之陷入混乱。由于千目的眼睛遍布其周身，对于心智屏障不够强大的冒险者而言，千目是极度危险的生物。

与其魔法能力相比，千目的物理猎杀能力较弱。不过考虑到被扰动的猎物几乎没有抵抗能力，这也许是一种合理的进化方向。
