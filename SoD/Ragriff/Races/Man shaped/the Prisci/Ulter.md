:::lang en

[[the Peírama]] originally advocated for the descendants of those who migrated south, whose builds differ subtly from the humans of the [[Lukatýsrika]] region: short but not stocky, lean but not agile.

Although they inhabit almost exclusively the north bank of Lukatýsrika, separated from [[Katafýgio]] by only [[Mountains of Yore]], there are few records of contact with the people of Katafýgio.

always stands on the north bank gazing toward [[flēre Forest]], for that is where Ulter comes closest to the remembered glory.

*After witnessing a hurricane, even a small butterfly will yearn for the day when the gales rise again.*

:::lang zh

Ulter: 拉丁语，意为“那一边的”。


[[the Peírama|失落的佩拉莫]] 最初主张南迁子民的后裔，形态与[[Lukatýsrika|卢卡特斯里卡]] 一带的人类有着微妙性的区别：矮小却不壮实，瘦削却不轻盈。

尽管几乎只栖息于卢卡特斯里卡的北岸，与[[Katafýgio|卡塔费格奥]] 仅相隔[[Mountains of Yore|旧日群山]] ，但鲜有与卡塔费格奥之民往来的记录。

总是执着于站在北岸向[[flēre Forest|哭泣森林]] 眺望，因为那是乌尔特距离追忆中的辉煌最接近的地方。
