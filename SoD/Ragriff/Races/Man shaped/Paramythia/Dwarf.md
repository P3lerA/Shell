:::lang en

A short, stocky race, the Dwarves mainly inhabit [[Ostia]]. They are always bearded and irritable. Unlike other races who enjoy basking in the sun, Dwarves prefer to dig for endless wealth underground.

Dwarves are masters of forging, possessing certain forge magic unknown even to elves, and they mine a peculiar metal known as [[Argingo Ore]].

:::

:::lang zh

身形矮小，体格敦实的种族，主要分布于[[Ostia|奥斯提亚]] ，总是满脸胡须，脾气暴躁。与其他乐意在阳光下沐浴的种族不同，矮人们更乐于在地下挖掘无尽的财富。

矮人精通锻造，掌握着一些连精灵都不知晓的锻造魔法，开采着一种名为[[Argingo Ore|靛银矿]] 的奇特金属。
