:::lang en

Small in stature, with exaggeratedly pointed and long ears and grayish-brown skin, this is a cunning species. Their name, Fallax, means *the deceitful*.

Like the [[Dwarf]], the Fallax is also proficient in smithing, but in a distinctly different manner.

Fallax are not particularly skilled at using [[Argingo Ore]], such a beautiful mineral, to forge exquisite weapons. The strength of Fallax weapons often lies in certain special properties, which are endowed by Fallax's unique magical system.

Although Fallax are often cunning and deceitful, they possess a strong sense of contract, considering it unforgivable to break a contract.

Fallax mostly inhabit [[Sylvatia]], though their figures can sometimes be seen in [[NR]] as well.

:::
