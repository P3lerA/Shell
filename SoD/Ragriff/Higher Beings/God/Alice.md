:::lang en

Native of [[the Peírama]]. The weary God of Time, the very first God. Her original name as a mortal is **Tháleia**. 

### Authorities

Time: possess memories from every loop, but lose the [temporality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temporality).

Fate: can enumerate any entity’s future.

*..I'm truly sorry for letting you misunderstand — what is called a "prophecy" is merely a prophet's self-indulgence; what emerges from my mouth is fate.*

*Has eyes that can see through everything, but no hands to solve it all…*

:::
