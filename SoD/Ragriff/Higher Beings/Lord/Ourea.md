:::lang en

Born from the mountains, the noble Lord. He has governed the [[Hillkind Mountains]] for over a thousand years, serving as the incarnation of the Lord whom the Hillkind revere. Among the [[Hillkind]], his name is "Behemoth".

2025.1.26 In order to harmonize the origins of the four World Lords, this entry has been renamed from Behemoth to Ourea. When you encounter the name Behemoth, please note the distinction — some places may not have been updated.
