:::lang en

A supernatural force that has penned countless tales. Within the vast majority of creations exists [[Mana]], and through some form of directing this power, magic is formed.

Magic is mainly divided into two categories: Pure Magic and Elemental Magic. As the name suggests, the former harnesses pure Mana, while the latter tends to use Mana to manipulate the [[Four Elements]]—water, fire, wind, and mountains.


*From the unreal to the real, from the darkness to the light, from the mortal to the immortal.*

:::lang zh

书写了无数故事的超自然力量。绝大多数造物体内都存在着[[Mana|魔力]] ，通过某种形式驱役魔力，便形成了魔法。

魔法主要分为两类：纯粹魔法与元素魔法。正如其名，前者所驱役的是纯粹的魔力，而后者则倾向以魔力驱役[[Four Elements|四元素]]——水，火，风以及群山。


*从虚幻到真实，从黑暗到光明，从死亡到不朽。*
