:::lang en

An ancient magic for imposing [[Curse]] upon a target, invented by [[Daemon R. Brigid]] in the primordial age.

Execrantia that directly manipulates fate is one of the most dangerous magics in the world, its danger even exceeding [[Mnemonia]]. Fortunately, since Brigid ascended to godhood, he has rarely appeared among people, and this magic has hardly been disseminated.

*Manipulating fate… I cannot imagine anything more dangerous than this.*

:::
