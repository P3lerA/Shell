:::lang en

A unique magic learned by elves from the blessing of [[Oraculum]], which allows elves to communicate with forests.

[[Wood Elf]] are particularly skilled at this type of magic, and can even use the manipulation of plant roots and branches for attack and defense.

:::

从[[Oraculum|圣树 欧拉克兰]] 的赐福中习得的，精灵独有的魔法，精灵可凭此与林木交流。

[[Wood Elf|木精灵]] 尤善此类魔法，甚至可以通过操纵植物的根枝进行攻击和防御。
