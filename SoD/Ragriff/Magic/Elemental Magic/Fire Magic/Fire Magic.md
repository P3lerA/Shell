:::lang en

Compared to [[Wind Magic]] and [[Mountain Magic]], it reads as a more standard elemental magic of Ragriff, requiring fire or a fire source to cast. Even so, there has never been a shortage of people obsessed with it in the world —after all, who could fail to be moved by the raging infernos kindled by your own hands?
