:::lang en

Southern Magic Association (hereinafter SMA), like [[M.A.R]], is an association aimed at popularizing and standardizing magic.

The difference is that SMA was originally a small magical research organization founded by [[Versatilys Sellano]] in 1353, which, under the former's political leadership, gradually developed over several decades into an entity that directs the magical development of [[Sylvatia]] and [[Lukatýsrika]].

As mentioned above, SMA's primary administrative targets are countries and powers in [[Sylvatia]] and [[Lukatýsrika]], with the following primary responsibilities:

1. Research and Optimization of Magic
2. [[Management of Dangerous Magic]] 
3. The Spread and Popularization of Magic

SMA was initially established in [[Zeska]]. In 1363, Versatilys Sellano, seeking to escape political constraints and for various other considerations, decided to relocate the headquarters to [[Puteoli]].

Today, the headquarters of SMA remains in Puteoli, and it also has a branch in [[Lukatýsrika]].

:::

南方魔法联盟, (下称SMA) , 与[[M.A.R|拉格里弗魔法协会]] 类似，意在普及，规范魔法的协会。

不同之处在于，SMA最初只是[[Versatilys Sellano|维萨第拉斯•塞拉诺]] 于1353年所成立的小型魔法研究组织，在前者身为政治家的领导力下于几十年间逐渐发展为统领[[Sylvatia|希尔瓦提亚]] 与[[Lukatýsrika|卢卡特斯里卡]] 魔法发展的存在。

正如上文所述，SMA的主要管缮目标是[[Sylvatia|希尔瓦提亚]] 及[[Lukatýsrika|卢卡特斯里卡]] 的国家与势力，其主要职责如下：

1. 魔法的研究与优化
2. [[Management of Dangerous Magic|危险魔法的管理]]
3. 魔法的传播与普及

SMA最初成立于[[Zeska|泽斯卡]] 。1363年，维萨拉第斯为了摆脱政治的束缚以及其他种种考虑，决定迁本部至[[Puteoli|普特奥利]] 。

如今，SMA的本部仍位于普特奥利，亦在[[Lukatýsrika|卢卡特斯里卡]] 设有分部。
