:::lang en

The Magic Association of Ragriff, aka the MARCH Association (referred to as the M.A.R. below), was established by the ArchMage [[Ryne Lynne]] in 1230 in [[Singhui]], with the purpose of popularizing and standardizing magic.

The regulatory target of M.A.R. is the countries and forces within [[Ragriff]]'s territory except [[Fidel]]. Its main duties are as follows:

1. Research and Optimization of Magic
2. [[Management of Dangerous Magic]]
3. The Spread and Popularization of Magic
4. Final Determination of [[ArchMage]]

M.A.R.'s headquarters to this day remains where it was established, Singhui. Additionally, the association has branches in most regions of [[Ragriff]]. They are located in: [[Hillkind Mountains]], [[South Alliance]], [[Gap place]], [[Angro]], [[Ostia]], [[Selene]].

*Notably, ****[[Sorcerer]]**** do not fall under M.A.R.'s jurisdiction.*

:::

:::lang zh

拉格里弗魔法协会，又称三月协会（下称M.A.R.协会，其简称，也是别称的由来），是由大魔法师[[Ryne Lynne|莱文•琳恩]] 于1230年在[[Singhui|新汇]] 建立的，意在普及，规范魔法的协会。

M.A.R.协会的管缮目标是[[Ragriff|拉格里弗]] 境内除[[Fidel|费德尔]] 以外的国家与势力。其主要职责如下：

1. 魔法的研究与优化
2. [[Management of Dangerous Magic|危险魔法的管理]] 
3. 魔法的传播与普及
4. [[ArchMage|大魔法师]] 的最终认定

M.A.R.协会的总部时至今日仍位于它成立的地方，新洲。此外，协会在[[Ragriff|拉格里弗]] 的大部分地域均设有分部。他们位于：[[Hillkind Mountains|山臣群山]] ，[[South Alliance|南方邦联]] ，[[Gap place|隙间地]] ，[[Angro|安格洛]] ，[[Ostia|奥斯提亚]] ，[[Selene|塞勒涅]] 。

*值得注意的是，**[[Sorcerer|魔法使们]]** 不归M.A.R.管。*

:::
