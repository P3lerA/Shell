:::lang en

A fairly common plant, short and sturdy, with many chambers within its branches, a gel secreted along the walls of each chamber.

By means of the gel it secretes internally along with a certain amount of Mana, it can materialize the structure of sound within the plant — recording the surrounding sounds.

:::
