:::lang en

The archipelago on the western side of Ragriff, where the [[Althea]] regime is located, has close exchanges with the Ragriff mainland.

When the first elves landed on it, time flowed to July of a certain year during the Sprouting Period, and thus the July Archipelago received its name. From this, [[Althea]] began.
