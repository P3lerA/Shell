:::lang en

The long river that originates from [[Mountain System Dol Guidor]], its upper reaches are generally referred to as the section from [[Eltarnern]] to [[Twilight Forest]].

Due to [[The Pale Night]], the upstream river water is entirely white and contains deadly poison.

Flowing northward, elves once migrated along its course.

:::
