:::lang en

A river located in southern Ragriff, flowing through Selene; a major tributary of [[Anduin Downstream]]. Elwing means *scattered starlight*.

When the northward-migrating Elves passed by, they saw the starlight reflected in the water, and thus the River Elwing got its name.

:::
