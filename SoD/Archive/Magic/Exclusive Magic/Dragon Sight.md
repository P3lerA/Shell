:::lang en

The gaze of dragons carries magical power, and through it, most dragons are able to unleash unique magic through their line of sight.

Considering that prolonged staring at dragons is often perceived by them as unfriendly behavior, countless explorers who were unaware of this fact paid a heavy price for it.

:::
