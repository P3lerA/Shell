:::lang en

Magic that preserves the "state" of objects, for example, keeping fresh green leaves from wilting.

There is still no universal interpretation regarding the valid targets of Preservation Magic. Generally speaking, Preservation Magic has no effect on targets that are considered 'alive' in the conventional sense, such as animals and humans.

:::
