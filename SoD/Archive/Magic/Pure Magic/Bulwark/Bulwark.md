:::lang en

A magic invented by [[Versatilys Sellano]], which was continuously improved through research by [[SMA]].

Magic that creates [[Phase Inverse]] mana and forms a structured pattern. Unlike [[Shield Spell]], Bulwark distributes incoming impacts evenly like an arch bridge when struck, thereby achieving far superior high-density attack defense performance compared to the Shield Spell.

The disadvantage of this approach is that Bulwark cannot be wrapped around the user like the Shield Spell does. This is because the magic that constitutes a barrier has a structured pattern, causing barriers to necessarily take on regular geometric shapes (refer to the principle of crystals).
