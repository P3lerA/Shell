:::lang en

Even among elemental magic, Mountain Magic can be said to be quite special. After all, the definition of "mountain" is extremely broad, and the plains seen everywhere can be considered extensions of "mountains", so not only does Mountain Magic possess tremendous power, it can even be cast in most places without special catalysts — sounding almost like a strengthened version of [[Wind Magic]]. Its drawbacks are practically self-evident: Mountain Magic is extremely precise and complex, often requiring unreasonable amounts of mana, and without the bloodline of [[Hillkind]], it's not as easy to use as it might appear.

:::lang zh

即使在元素魔法中山魔法也可以说是相当特别的一种。毕竟“山”的定义是非常广泛的，随处可见的平原都可以视作“山”的延续，因此山魔法在具有强大威力的同时甚至可以在大部分地方无需特别的触媒而释放，听起来简直像[[Wind Magic|风魔法]] 的加强版。其缺点简直呼之欲出：山魔法是非常精密而复杂的，许多时候都需要不可理喻的魔力，倘若没有[[Hillkind|山臣]] 的血脉，这玩意并不像看起来那么好用。
