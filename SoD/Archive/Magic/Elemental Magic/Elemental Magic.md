:::lang en

The four great elements that constitute **existence** are: Wind, Mountain, Water, and Fire. Elemental Magic is the practice of wielding these four elements through magic, forming the four corresponding types of magic.

For a long time, elemental magic was overlooked by the major magic associations due to its lack of practicality, until the ArchMage [[Keaperra Thurandare]] established a systematic elemental magic system, making it more accessible and more feasible.

:::lang zh

构成**存在**的四大元素[Element]是：风，山，水，火。元素魔法即以魔法驾驭这四种元素，构成的对应的四种魔法。

长期以来，元素魔法都由于缺乏实用性而没有受到各大魔法协会的重视，直至大魔法师[[Keaperra Thurandare|加贝拉•索兰达尔]] 创建了系统的元素魔法体系，使之门槛更低，可行性更高。
