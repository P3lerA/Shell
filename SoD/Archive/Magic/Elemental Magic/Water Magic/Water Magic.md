Compared with [[Wind Magic]] and [[Mountain Magic]], it is more like a standard Ragriff elemental magic, and can only be cast where water exists. Even so, there has never been a shortage of people obsessed with it in the world — after all, who could not be fascinated by the waves summoned by your hand?
