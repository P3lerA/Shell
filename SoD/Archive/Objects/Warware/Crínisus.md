:::lang en

A longsword forged by the magic of the [[Common Elf|Common Elves]]; its blade takes visible form only when it touches flesh and blood. It was once the ceremonial sword of [[Clan Mysterion]] in their mystery rites.

At the center of the hilt a faded hexagram can be seen, ringed by a few barely legible fragments of inscription. It feels surprisingly heavy in the hand.

:::
