:::lang en

Following the [[Origin]] of the [[Hillkind]], the God of Fate Godo fell, and his scattered authority manifested as a brilliant meteor. [[Scipio Hiereus]] witnessed the brilliance of the meteor and, inspired by it, created [[Astreia]].

:::

:::lang zh

即 [[Hillkind|山臣]] 的 [[Origin|起源]]后，命运之神戈多陨落，他的权柄散落所显现出的灿烂的流星。 [[Scipio Hiereus|西庇阿·希琉斯]] 见证了流星的光辉，受到启发，从而创造了 [[Astreia|星辰魔法]] 。

:::
