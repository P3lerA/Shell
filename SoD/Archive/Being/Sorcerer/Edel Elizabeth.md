:::lang en

Edel Elizabeth (?), [[Eltarnern]] native, a High Elf Sorceress from the archaic period, whose innate magic is [[Mnemonia]], authored [[Where Had She Gone]].

Although she possessed the talent to become a TreeKeeper, Edel chose to leave rather than be imprisoned for life. She traveled north along the [[Anduin River]] until reaching a coastal plain, which is today's [[Forgotten Plain]], where she lived peacefully for over a hundred years.

The peaceful life was disrupted by [[Halfdrake]] expanding their territory. This evil species, possessing magic, intelligence, and massive scale, was spurned by their own kind and attempted to expand outward. After a long and arduous battle, Edel exiled this species to the [[Oceanus]].

However, during this battle, Edel was also grievously wounded, and she perished not long after. The magic that emanated from her became the origin of the Forgotten Plain's effect of causing people to forget the past.

:::

:::lang zh

艾蒂露•伊丽莎白[Edel•Elizabeth] (?) ，[[Eltarnern|艾尔达南]] 人，生活在荒古时期的高精灵魔法使，天赋魔法为[[Mnemonia|记忆魔法]] ，著有[[Where Had She Gone|她往何处去？]] 。

虽有成为圣树存护者的天赋，但不愿为此禁锢自己一生的艾蒂露选择出走。她沿着[[Anduin River|埃多因河]] 一路北上，直至一片临海的平原，即今天的[[Forgotten Plain|遗忘平原]] ，在那里，她安然生活了百余年。

宁适的生活被扩张领地的[[Halfdrake|半身龙]] 所打破，这种拥有魔力，智慧以及巨大规模的邪恶物种因不受同族待见，试图向外扩张领地。在漫长而艰苦的战斗后，艾蒂露将这一物种放逐至大陆外侧的边境之海。

然而，在这场战斗中艾蒂露亦身受重伤，此后不久便身陨于此，所散发出的魔力是遗忘平原使人遗忘过去这一效应的由来。

:::
