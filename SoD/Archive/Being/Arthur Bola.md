:::lang en

Arthur Bola (2886-2919), poet, [[Althea]] native, talented yet died young.

Bola, who came from a well-to-do family, did not wallow in pleasure. On the contrary, after meeting the love of his life, the two of them retired to a cabin at the edge of [[Cerulean Forest]], where Bola devoted himself to composing poetry.

Happy lives are mostly alike, while each misfortune is unique. When his lover was humiliated by outsiders, the enraged Arthur Bola initiated a duel he was destined to lose.

The miracle did not happen, and thus a genius perished. But perhaps he still left us a legacy unknown to the world.

:::

阿尔蒂尔•波拉[Arthur •Bola]（2886-2919），诗人，[[Althea|阿莱西亚]] 人，才华横溢却英年早逝。

出身于优渥家庭的波拉并没有沉溺于享乐，恰恰相反，在结识了一生的挚爱后，两人便隐居于秘林边缘的木屋中，在那里波拉潜心于创作诗歌。

幸福的生活大都相似，不幸却各有各的不同。当他的恋人被外人羞辱时，怒不可遏的波拉发起了一次必败的决斗。

奇迹并没有发生，一位天才就此陨落了。但也许，他仍给我们留下了不为人知的遗产。
