:::lang en

A clan existing within the [[Elf]]s that divines destiny, revealing the guidance of [[Mnemosyne]] and mastering the esoteric [[Astreia]].

Though few in number, they have produced many famous sorcerers.

It is worth noting that the Clan of Hiereus is merely a collective term in modern times. In the era when [[Oraculum]] was still flourishing, the Clan of Hiereus was singular, but after the final division between [[Common Elf]] and [[High Elf]], the Common Elves established their own unique priest institution.
