:::lang en

A priestly clan of the [[Common Elf]], recent but scholarly in spirit. After [[Althea]] was established, the Common Elves chose the Grey elf clan of Mysterion to reveal guidance. Mysterion means *secret rites*.

Since being chosen, Clan Mysterion has contributed significantly to the research of [[Astreia]]. The prophecies they have revealed are also more concise and understandable compared to [[Clan Hiereus]].

:::

:::lang zh

[[Common Elf|亚精灵]] 的祭司家族，晚近而富学术精神。[[Althea|阿莱西亚]] 成立后，亚精灵选中了米斯特利恩这一灰精灵家族以揭示指引。米斯特利恩意为* 秘仪。*

自被选中以来，米斯特利恩家族已为 [[Astreia|星辰魔法]]的研究作出了颇多贡献。他们所揭示的谶语，较[[Clan Hiereus|希琉斯家族]] 也更加简明易懂。
