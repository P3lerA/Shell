:::lang en

[[Munitio]]'s legate who carries out **fear**. They generally appear at the world's borders, such as [[Oceanus]]. They manifest in different forms depending on the circumstances, and their appearance generally triggers an uncontrollable, instinctive fear in [[Being]]. For example, when someone tries to sail from the border sea toward the outer sea, when they travel too far, they can see what appears to be the wreckage of a sunken vessel—the description of someone who saw that thing, felt overwhelming terror, and turned back because of it. Most of those who continued forward never returned.

:::

:::lang zh

[[Munitio|慕尼第奥]] 的，代行**恐惧**的眷属。一般出现在世界的边境，如[[Oceanus|俄刻阿诺斯]] 。在不同的情景中以不同的形态出现，其形态一般会引起[[Being|造物]] 本能的无法抑制的恐惧。例如，当有人试图自边境之海驶向外海时，当他行驶得太远，就能看到某种似乎是沉没船只残骸的东西——这是看到那种东西，进而感到无比恐惧并因此返航的人的描述。继续前进的人，大多没能活着回来。

:::
