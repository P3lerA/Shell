### the Legates

[[Legate]]

In this world, Gods are more like rulers under Majesty, still retaining human desires.

Beings of a higher plane that more strongly manifest divinity, such as [[Ourea]], are called Lord. They are generally products of the world compilation process itself, existing beyond human cognition, and what is seen in Ragriff is mostly their avatars.

Textor Fati are an even more abstract concept, more akin to tuners of causality, but not in any way perceptible to mortals.

The Legates act on behalf of the Avatars of Will, exercising abstract wills through concrete forms.

### 眷属

[[Legate|意志眷属]]

在这个世界中，受位者更像是莫杰斯塔的执政，仍然保有人的渴望。

更高位面的，更表现出神性的存在，例如[[Ourea|俄瑞亚]] 则称为主。他们一般是世界编译进程本身的产物，高于人的认知而存在，在拉格里弗中所见的大多是他们的化身。

编织者则是更抽象的概念，他们更像是调律者，但并不是以人所能认知的，显式的方式。

眷属是意志化身的代行者，以具象的形态行使抽象的意志。
