:::lang en

After [[Duel on the Plains]], Ferdinand confesses most of the truth to Epypsyny, including his real name, after which he sets off on a journey. After Keaperra Thurandare's death, [[Clan Thurandare]] declined rapidly. Given how rare the surname Thurandare has become, the protagonist can easily guess that there might be some connection between himself and that great ArchMage. When he asks Ferdinand about this, he briefly explains the events recounted in [[Rock Beneath the Riddle-Prologue]], and tells Epypsyny that he thinks all those pursuing them are looking in the wrong direction. Since none of the causal chains regarding the murder hold up, Thurandare most likely was not killed by Emporiae, who therefore would not be hiding in the Hillkind Mountains out of a desire to escape punishment. If Emporiae is still alive, he must be living in some beautiful, remote place.

While passing through [[Mountains of Tide]], Epypsyny recalled Ferdinand's description of "scenic and secluded," and decided to try to find the truth of Deep Stone Below here.

After arriving at [[TideWatch]] (the flat top of the Mountains of Tide makes it ideal for watching the tides, hence the name), using Sight Magic allows you to discover additional construction beneath the platform. If it is at night, you will directly enter a boss battle with Emporiae. The outcome of the boss battle only affects a few lines of subsequent dialogue and has no impact on story progression (it is virtually impossible to win this early in the game).

:::lang zh

[[Duel on the Plains|原野上的剑斗]] 后，斐迪南向玊初坦白了大部分真相，包括他的本名，后者从此踏上旅途。自加贝拉•索兰达尔之死后，[[Clan Thurandare|索兰达尔家族]] 迅速败落，考虑到如今索兰达尔是一个非常罕见的姓氏，主角很容易联想到自己与那位了不起的大魔法师是否存在某种联系。向斐迪南问及此事时，他会简单阐述[[Rock Beneath the Riddle-Prologue|深岩之下 谜 引子]] 中提及的往事，并告诉玊初他认为那些搜捕的人都走错了方向。既然诸多关于谋杀的因果都无法成立，那么索兰达尔很可能并非恩波利亚所杀，后者自然也不会抱着逃罪的心理藏身于山臣群山一带，如果恩波利亚还在世，一定是隐居于某个景色优美且人迹罕至的地方。

在途径[[Mountains of Tide|望潮山脉]] 时，玊初想起斐迪南关于“景色优美且人迹罕至”的描述，决定试图在这里寻找深岩之下的真相。

在到达[[TideWatch|观潮平台]] 后（望潮山脉顶部平整，适于观潮，因此得名），使用显形魔法就可以发现平台下另有构造。如果是夜间就会直接进入与恩波利亚的boss战，boss战的胜负只影响后续几句对话的台词，对剧情发展没有影响（前期几乎没有可能打得过）。
