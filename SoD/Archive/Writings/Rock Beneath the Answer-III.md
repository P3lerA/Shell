:::lang en

Players who arrive with a purpose can easily recognize that what is claimed in [[Ramesses' Journal]] is not fact. (The purpose of players researching this matter is to find the beginning of the contradiction between [[Clan Thurandare]] and [[Clan Sylva]].)

The [[Morgras Sylva’s Record]] found in the ruined hut not far from the mine tunnels points out two unreasonable aspects: if using pure magic, destroying the mine's main support would require an enormous amount of magical power, and Sellano clearly does not have that much; however, if using mountain magic, the required magical power would be far less, and among the four people, Thurandare, being most closely associated with elemental magic, is the most suspicious. Additionally, the [[Sellano at the Turning Point]] was likely written around 1365–1367, at which time the SMA headquarters had already moved to Puteoli, and Sellano and Thurandare were far apart—unable to even see each other—making it extremely unreasonable for Sellano to coerce Thurandare into writing flattering things about him, which clearly does not align with the "cautious and scheming" image of Sellano described in [[Ramesses' Journal]]. From this, it is highly likely that Thurandare voluntarily wrote the book, under a false name so as to frame the unwitting Sellano and thereby console Ramesses, who had achieved nothing — it was not his own incompetence, he had merely been betrayed by an old friend (Ramesses and Thurandare eventually married).

It must be said that the two reasons above are not sufficient to firmly establish that everything was a scheme of Thurandare's. However, the matter dates back far, and furthermore, if the above assumptions hold, the origin of the conflict between Clan Thurandare and Clan Sylva would be accounted for. Thus, it can be considered the most reasonable explanation.

The story is drawing to a close here, with the death of Archmage [[Keaperra Thurandare]] basically completing the loop. Players can choose to continue exploring or end the quest here.

:::

:::lang zh

带着目的而来的玩家很容易认识到，[[Ramesses' Journal|拉美西斯的手记]] 中所称并非事实。（玩家研究此事的目的是寻找[[Clan Thurandare|索兰达尔家族]] 与[[Clan Sylva|风语家族]] 矛盾的开端）

在矿道不远处的残破小屋中所找到的[[Morgras Sylva’s Record|莫格拉斯•风语的记录]] 会指出两处不合理之处：倘若使用纯粹魔法，破坏矿区的主支撑需要巨大的魔力量，塞拉诺显然没有那么多；但如果使用群山魔法，所需的魔力量便会小得多，四人中，与元素魔法关系最紧密的索兰达尔最可疑。此外，[[Sellano at the Turning Point|历史转折中的塞拉诺]] 的成书时间大概是1365-1367年，此时SMA本部已迁移至普特奥利，塞拉诺与索兰达尔两人相距甚远，在甚至看不到对方的情况下胁迫索兰达尔为自己著书美言显然是极不合理的，与[[Ramesses' Journal|拉美西斯的手记]] 中描述的塞拉诺“谨慎多谋”的形象显然并不吻合。由此，极有可能是索兰达尔自愿成书，冠以假名，便于栽赃毫不知情的塞拉诺，以此宽慰一事无成的拉美西斯——那并非他的无能，只是被老友陷害（拉美西斯与索兰达尔最终成为夫妻）。

必须要说，上述两条理由并不足以咬定一切都是索兰达尔的谋划，但此事年代已远，加之如果上述假设成立，索兰达尔家族与风语家族矛盾的起源将得到解释，可以认为这是最合理的解释。

到此为止，故事已经接近尾声，大魔法师[[Keaperra Thurandare|加贝拉•索兰达尔]] 之死基本完成了闭环。玩家可以选择继续探索，或者就此结束任务。
