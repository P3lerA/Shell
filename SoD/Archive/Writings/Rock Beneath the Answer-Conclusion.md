:::lang en

[[Tar Ramesses]] is a talented, weak, and ordinary person. In a sense, it was precisely his weakness that led to the events that followed.

In this story, [[Versatilys Sellano]] is nothing short of a perfect man. His genius, his tolerance, his foresight came at the cost of four people's tragedy, traded for the bright future of magic in the southern kingdoms. You might ask: then why not bring him along to restart? The reasonable explanation is this: his talent as a mage made it possible, his stratagems as a statesman brought about the turning point, but this is all he is. He is an extraordinary person, nothing more. He cannot transcend the identities he was given, cannot pursue the sublime that exists on a higher plane—something fundamentally beyond human cognition. Still, this does not diminish the remarkable undertaking and precious legacy he accomplished.

You don't need me to parse it to see that [[Cleopatra Thurandare]] is an incredibly twisted person. To keep the one she admired, she resorted to every means possible. However, the little conscience she had left kept her from falling into the abyss—it was precisely this impure sense of good and evil that brought the guilt that would torment her for the rest of her life. After designing the murder of Tar Ramesses' father, she couldn't face him and went north alone to study. Her inner turmoil even led her to enchant the tunnel manager to warn irrelevant miners about safety.

Although not reflected in the story, it should be said that [[Elvin Sylva]] is a highly talented sorcerer. The richness of magic in his blood is so potent that even his direct descendant, [[Morgras Sylva]], is also a sorcerer. His purity caused the public to mistakenly believe he had betrayed his oath of allegiance to SMA, and he suffered severe stigmatization in Fallax society. In 1396, Elvin chose to end his life.

No matter who, whether outstanding or not, who committed the crimes and who achieved the merits, each with their own differences — no one has a perfect ending. Self-pity, sin, sorrow, unwilling to endure humiliation — this story is undoubtedly just one insignificant strand among countless cycles of tragedy. However, had this twisted story never occurred, the Thurandare clan and the Sylva clan would never have become enemies, and perhaps [[Keaperra Thurandare]] would not have died young, and perhaps the protagonist would have grown up to be a happy, ordinary person. Then, who will come to sever this painful cycle…

:::lang zh

[[Tar Ramesses|塔•拉美西斯]] 是一个有才能的，软弱的，平凡的人。从某种意义上来说，正是他的软弱导致了后来的故事。

在这个故事中，[[Versatilys Sellano|维萨第拉斯•塞拉诺]] 简直是一个完人。他的天才，他的宽容，他的深谋以四人的悲剧为代价，换取了南方诸国魔法事业的光明未来。你也许会问：那怎么不拉他去重启呢？合理的解释是：他身为魔法师的才能提供了可能，他身为政治家的谋策带来了转机，但这就是他的全部。他是一个了不起的人，仅此而已。他不能超越那些他被赋予的身份，追求更高位面的，简直脱离了身为人的认知的崇高。不过，这并不影响他所完成的了不起的事业与珍贵的遗产。

不用我解析你也能看出来，[[Cleopatra Thurandare|克里奥佩特拉•索兰达尔]] 是一个无比扭曲的人。为了留住自己倾慕之人，她无所不用其极。然而，她仅有的一点良知使她悬于深渊之上——正是并不纯粹的善恶，带来了折磨她终生的罪恶感。设计杀死拉美西斯的父亲后，她不敢面对他而孤身一人北上求学，心中的不安甚至让她蛊惑了矿道的管理人以提醒无关的矿工注意安全。

虽然故事中并没有体现，但应当说，[[Elvin Sylva|埃尔文•风语]] 是一名卓有天赋的魔法使。他血中的魔力之浓，甚至其直系后代[[Morgras Sylva|莫格拉斯•风语]] 亦是魔法使。他的纯粹使公众误以为他背叛了效忠于SMA的契约，在妖精社会中饱受污名，1396年，埃尔文选择了自尽。

无论是谁，杰出与否，孰罪孰功，各与不同，没有人拥有圆满的结局。自怨自艾，罪恶，悲哀，不甘受辱，这个故事无疑是无数循环的悲剧中微不足道的一支。然而，倘若没有这个扭曲的故事，索兰达尔家族与风语家族便不会结仇，也许因此[[Keaperra Thurandare|加贝拉•索兰达尔]] 便不会英年早逝，也许主角便会是幸福成长的平凡之人。那么，又有谁来将这苦痛的循环斩断呢…
