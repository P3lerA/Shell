:::lang en

The ability granted to a [[God]]. A single, clearly defined power.

For example, the authority of Fate is the ability to enumerate any entity's possible futures. The God of Fate does not control fate.

:::

:::lang zh

即 [[God|受位者]] 被赋予的能力。简单，明确的一项能力。例如：“命运”的权柄是枚举任一实体未来的能力。命运之神并不能操控命运。

:::
