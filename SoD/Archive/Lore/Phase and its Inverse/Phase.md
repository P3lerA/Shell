:::lang en

All things possess their Phase. "Phase" can be understood as an attribute of things—for example, the elements within a matrix are the matrix's Phase.

:::

:::lang zh

事物皆有其相。“相”可以理解为事物的属性，比如，矩阵中的元素是矩阵的相。

:::
