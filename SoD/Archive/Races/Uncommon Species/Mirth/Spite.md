:::lang en

A subspecies of Mirth, born from pure malice. Without using [[Sight Magic]] to force the exposure of its shattered body, there is no significant difference in appearance between Spite and Mirth.

In the midst of long-standing hatred, they have forgotten how to use [[Mirthery]] and instead evolved [[Hex]].

Before [[Sunglow Tower]] was inverted, it was only visible in [[Detholock]]. Afterward, it became visible within [[NR]].

:::
