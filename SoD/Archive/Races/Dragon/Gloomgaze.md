:::lang en

A dangerous species with powerful magic, adept at various [[Dragon Sight]], and because their eyes are dim and their eye sockets sunken, making it difficult to discern what they are gazing at, they are excellent hunters.

Commonly found in [[Mount Angro]], with small amounts also visible in the southwest of [[SingLand]]'s homeland.

:::
