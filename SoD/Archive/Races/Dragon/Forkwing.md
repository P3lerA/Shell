:::lang en

Dragon whose wings are split into two at the tail end, hence the name, with relatively strong flight capability and relatively weak magical power.

It has a very docile temperament and is the only subspecies to have been domesticated on a large scale. It has a close relationship with [[Clan Anngrown]], serving as a mount for the clan's senior members.

Commonly found in [[Mount Angro]], and can be seen in many places.

:::

:::lang zh

双翼于尾端裂为两翼的龙，因此得名，飞行能力较强，魔力较弱。

性情非常温顺，是唯一被大规模驯服的亚种。与[[Clan Anngrown|安戈蒙家族]] 关系密切，是家族中居于高位者的坐骑。

多见于[[Mount Angro|安格洛山]] ，在许多地方都可见。

:::
