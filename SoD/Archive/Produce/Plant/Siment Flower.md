:::lang en

Siment Flower can suppress the surrounding Mana; the larger the plant, the stronger this effect. It is an extremely rare and dangerous plant. Unlike [[Ashfield Grass]], which "cannot grow in places with Mana," Siment Flower actively makes magic difficult to use in its vicinity.

Additionally, the nectar of the Siment Flower is a high-performance type of [[Suppressant]].

:::
